Pillar 03 · Smart Execution

Smart Execution

You can train for years and still not know if you’re getting better. Most people never check.

What It Is

Smart Execution is the pillar that turns effort into evidence. Without a feedback loop, you’re guessing — and guessing feels a lot like progress right up until it isn’t.

The Method — TRACK

A short loop that shows what’s working, what’s not, and what to adjust:

  • T — Timeline: when you check, on a fixed cadence, not “eventually.”
  • R — Record Progress: what actually happened, in numbers where possible.
  • A — Analyze Gaps: where the record and the goal disagree.
  • C — Corrective Action: the one change that closes the gap, not five.
  • K — Keep Iterating: run it again next cycle.

The Tools

The same scorecard from GO FAST, a fifteen-minute weekly review, and the willingness to change the plan instead of defending it.

Where It Shows Up

A practice log that gets reviewed, not just filled in. A weekly check that catches a plateau in week three instead of month three. A correction made while it’s still cheap to make.
